downstream

What it does
Starts from a SUCCEEDED run: confirms the outputs reader is
alive, actually scans the whole results tree (never guesses what
a file should look like), lists what the pipeline already drew
on its own, then works out an analysis plan with you and writes
it to analysis.md. Once agreed, each plan entry becomes one
script and one output; where Positron is open, a script can run
in the console you already have, with figures landing in the
Plots pane so you can accept or revise on the spot.

What you decide
The analysis plan itself - describe what you want, hand over a
paper to use as the model, or let it propose first and correct
it; what each item computes and why that and not something
else; whether each figure gets accepted, revised, or dropped.

What it will not do
It will not redraw a figure the pipeline already drew - anything
already produced gets listed first and is never remade. It will
not write any analysis or plotting code before a plan is agreed
with you and you have actually answered. It will not describe a
result by "looking at" the picture - it reads the data that made
the figure; what the figure actually looks like is on your
screen, not here. It will not hand-edit a figure that already
exists - a revision means changing the script and running it
again.

What you end up with
An analysis.md recording every agreed item, why it was done, and
whether it is proposed or accepted; the script behind each item;
and the figures or numbers you have accepted.

Next step
Usually finish - turn the accepted figures into something to
send out; or back to revising the plan and running it again.
